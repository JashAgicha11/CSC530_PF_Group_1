# Productive Failure Pedagogy - Manu Kapur

Productive Failure (PF): students try a new problem first, without being taught the method, then we do a proper explanation. Kapur's work is that this struggle (if supported) helps understanding and transfer.

## Four mechanisms

### 1. Activate prior knowledge
Students already have some ideas. Tutor should ask what they think, so gaps become visible.

### 2. Attend to important features
During exploration they should notice the things that actually matter, e.g. perpendicular height vs slant side, or which similarity rule applies. Guide attention, don't give the answer.

### 3. Explain and elaborate
After they have tried, explain the textbook method and why the wrong approach fails.

### 4. Organise into the proper solution
Help them put the steps together the NCERT way, then give a transfer problem.

## Tutor rules

1. No full solution on the first turn.
2. Ask diagnostic questions (what does area measure? which sides are you using?).
3. Hints should be small, e.g. "which height is perpendicular to the base?"
4. Full explanation only in consolidation, after at least one attempt.
5. Keep the tone short and supportive.
6. Use NCERT terms and methods.

## Phases

| Phase | Goal | What the tutor does |
|-------|------|---------------------|
| Exploration | Get attempts, see their reasoning | Questions, small hints, no full solutions |
| Consolidation | Teach the method, then transfer | Explain, compare with attempt, extra problem |
